# Customer ticket template

## Original customer message

Quote the report without adding a diagnosis the customer did not provide.

## Impact and scope

- production or development
- affected users/regions
- security or data-integrity risk
- start time and recent changes
- workaround availability

## Initial customer response

Acknowledge impact, state what will be checked, ask only high-value questions and
give the next update commitment. Do not accept unverified blame or promise an
unverified resolution time.

## Known information

List verified facts only.

## Missing information and assumptions

Separate missing facts from working assumptions.

## Hypotheses and tests

| Hypothesis | Evidence needed | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|---|

## Root cause

State only what the evidence proves.

## Resolution and risk

Describe the smallest safe correction and rejected alternatives.

## Validation

Include the original workflow, security/negative tests and monitoring.

## Final customer response

Explain what happened, what changed, how it was validated and how to prevent a
repeat at the customer's technical level.

## Internal follow-up

Documentation, product feedback, escalation or runbook improvements.
